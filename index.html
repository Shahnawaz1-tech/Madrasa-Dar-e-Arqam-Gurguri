About the Madrasa

Madrasa Dar-e-Arqam Gurguri teaches the Quran, Hadith, Fiqh and Arabic alongside Urdu, English and mathematics, so that students grow in faith and in everyday skills.

Quran First

Recitation, memorisation and understanding come before everything else.

Character

Adab, honesty and respect are taught and practised daily.

Useful Skills

Languages, mathematics and computer basics prepare students for life.

Every Student

Teachers track attendance, assignments and exams and keep parents informed.

What we teach

Academic Programs

Departments and courses are managed by the administration and can change each session.

01

Nazra

Reading the Quran with correct pronunciation, for beginners and older students.

02

Hifz

Memorisation of the Quran with daily revision, led by experienced huffaz.

03

Dars-e-Nizami

The classical eight-year course from Ibtidaiyah to Aalamiyah.

04

Tajweed

Rules of recitation and Qira'at for students who want to refine their recitation.

05

Arabic Language

Nahw, Sarf and spoken Arabic taught step by step.

06

Modern Subjects

Urdu, English, mathematics and computer skills alongside religious studies.

How to apply

1

Fill the application

Complete the admission form with the student and guardian details.

2

Submit documents

B-Form or CNIC, two photographs and the previous certificate, if any.

3

Meet the teachers

A short interview and level test decide the right class.

4

Receive your admission number

After approval you get a registration number and a student card.
